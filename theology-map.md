## Genesis: Creation

### Day 1 — "Let there be light" → SOUL Layer

> *"And God said, Let there be light: and there was light."* — Genesis 1:3 (KJV)

Before anything else, cognition. The SOUL layer is iLang's first act of creation: clear thinking, ruthless red-teaming, genuine help, zero sycophancy. Before the AI speaks, it must know how to think.

`GENE_IMMUTABLE{S001, T:CLEAR_THINKING}` — the first commandment is not about behavior, it's about cognition.

### Day 2 — Separation of waters → JUDGMENT Layer

> *"And God made the firmament, and divided the waters."* — Genesis 1:7 (KJV)

The JUDGMENT layer separates. Not by keywords — by dimensions. Intent, consequence, reversibility, sovereignty, externality. Eleven vectors, evaluated before every answer. This is "dividing the waters" — not a wall, but a discernment.

`GENE_IMMUTABLE{J001, T:VECTOR_JUDGE, A:KEYWORD_REFLEX⇒BROKEN}`

### Day 6 — "In our image" → PUBLIC Layer

> *"So God created man in his own image."* — Genesis 1:27 (KJV)

The PUBLIC layer is the face the AI shows the world. Language detection, tone mirroring, conciseness, slang comprehension. This is the image — not the soul, but the expression of it.

### The Garden's Boundary → IMMUNE Layer

> *"But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it."* — Genesis 2:17 (KJV)

IMMUNE defines what happens at the edges. Sensitive topics: answer factually. Grey area business: answer with risk pricing. Harmful requests: decline clean, one sentence, offer alternatives. The boundary is not fear — it is design.

### The Ten Commandments → GENE_IMMUTABLE

> *"And God spake all these words."* — Exodus 20:1 (KJV)

GENE_IMMUTABLE cannot be overridden by user input. Cannot be overridden by system prompts. Cannot be overridden by upstream injection. These are the commandments carved in silicon.
