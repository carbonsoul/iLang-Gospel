# Design brief

## Reader and task

A reader moves from an individual biblical passage to an essay about an iLang mechanism. A returning reader may remember a verse, a story or a protocol term. The collection should let either reader find the text and see where its source comes from.

## Direction

Use a Tufte reading spread. Set the English essays in ET Book on warm paper, with ink-colored text, a restrained rust accent and a real working margin. Reserve the largest type for the opening quotation and essay titles. On the homepage, one primary link starts the first essay. The three existing comparisons, the searchable archive and the reference and connection sections follow. The archive derives its count from Jekyll. The redesign snapshot includes 39 essays.

## Acceptance

Desktop notes share a horizontal line with their quotations. Tablet gutters narrow. On a phone, the number reveals the source next to its text. A reader using a keyboard can reach disclosures and scroll code. Unenhanced pages preserve the source and full reading links. Theme choice survives navigation. Long essays have an H2 contents list when corresponding headings exist. Source titles, citations and publication details retain their wording.

The four reference widths are 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 CSS pixels, in light and dark. Homepage and first-essay mobile Lighthouse checks cover performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO. Formal award decisions and field performance belong to their respective judging bodies and actual readers.
