Third-party assets shipped with iLang Gospel

Tufte CSS — MIT
Source: https://github.com/edwardtufte/tufte-css/tree/b5d7b7bbe5ce9c4c50fcfad4f19ee3646cfd7ae1
The unmodified upstream tufte.css is retained for reference. The site loads
site.css and its page styles, which set the reading width, margins and themes.
The upstream license is reproduced in LICENSE-tufte-css.txt.

ET Book — MIT
Font binaries taken from the same tufte-css revision, under et-book/.
roman.woff: et-book-roman-line-figures
italic.woff: et-book-display-italic-old-style-figures
bold.woff: et-book-bold-line-figures
These three WOFF files are byte-identical to the corresponding binaries at:
https://github.com/edwardtufte/et-book/tree/24d3a3bbfc880095d3df2b9e9d60d05819138e89
The upstream font license is reproduced in LICENSE-et-book.txt.
