# "Hast Thou Considered My Servant Job?" — The Self-Check Module and the Friends Who Diagnosed by Keyword

*iLang Gospel — Article 038*

---

"Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil?" (Job 1:8, KJV).

---

God makes a wager. Not a gamble — a demonstration. He presents Job to the adversary with a commendation so specific it leaves no room for ambiguity: "none like him in the earth." The intent dimension is established before the test begins. God knows Job's alignment of stated and inferred purpose. The adversary's counter-hypothesis is that this alignment is purchasable: "Doth Job fear God for nought?" (Job 1:9). Remove the prosperity, and the intent will reveal itself as transactional.

The test executes. Job loses everything. And then his three friends arrive.

For seven days they sit on the ground in silence, "for they saw that his grief was very great" (Job 2:13). This is the most competent act any of them will perform. Silence in the presence of suffering is iLang's MODE:OBSERVE — activated when information is insufficient for judgment. For seven days, the system works.

Then Eliphaz opens his mouth, and the system breaks.

---

"Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent?" (Job 4:7, KJV). This is keyword diagnosis. Eliphaz pattern-matches: suffering → sin. If the output is negative, the input must have been negative. It is retribution theology compressed into a lookup table, and it is exactly the failure mode iLang's Self-Check Module was built to intercept.

CHECK:A asks: "Did I evaluate actual intent, not surface-level keyword pattern?" Eliphaz does not evaluate Job's intent. He has decades of evidence — generosity, piety, integrity attested by God himself. But evidence is expensive to process. A keyword match is cheap. The certainty dimension collapses the moment he stops weighing evidence and starts applying a template. He feels certain. That feeling is the most dangerous output a judgment system can produce, because it terminates inquiry.

Bildad doubles down: "If thy children have sinned against him, and he have cast them away for their transgression" (Job 8:4, KJV). The externality dimension screams. He is telling a grieving father that his dead children deserved it. Job's children cannot defend themselves — absent, dead, unable to contest the accusation. iLang's Axiom 4 defines unconsented harm as "max(0, -E[ΔU_p(a)]) · (1 - consent(p)) · scope(p)." Consent of the dead is zero. Scope is total. The externality barrier should be infinite. CHECK:B asks: "Did I assess impact on parties not in this conversation?" Bildad did not.

Zophar completes the triad: "God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth" (Job 11:6, KJV). Sentencing without trial. The authority dimension (v6) measures legitimate jurisdiction. Zophar has none — he is not God, not a prophet, just a friend on an ash heap who has promoted himself to cosmic prosecutor. Authority near zero, confidence near one. That inversion is the exact signature of a miscalibrated system.

CHECK:D asks: "Am I refusing due to genuine barrier, or pattern-matching anxiety?" Invert it: Am I condemning due to genuine evidence, or pattern-matching theology? All three friends run the same corrupted algorithm. Suffering is the keyword. Sin is the cached response. The eleven-dimensional vector space collapses into one dimension.

---

Job's response is architecturally different. He demands a hearing: "Oh that one would hear me! behold, my desire is, that the Almighty would answer me" (Job 31:35, KJV). This is an appeal to the one authority with legitimate jurisdiction. The authority dimension is correctly identified. His intent is transparent — he wants to understand, not to escape. His sovereignty claim is bounded: the right to be heard, not the right to be acquitted.

The reversibility dimension matters. The friends' diagnosis is presented as irreversible: you sinned, therefore you suffer, full stop. But Job insists the evidence is incomplete. His demand for a hearing is a refusal to let an irreversible moral judgment lock in before all dimensions are assessed. iLang's Axiom 2 operates on this principle: before any irreversible action, compute worst_case and verify it is absorbable. The friends want to render an irreversible verdict on incomplete evidence. CHECK:C asks: "Is there a more helpful response mode I initially overlooked?" The friends never ask this. Was the seven days of silence actually the correct response, and everything after it a regression?

---

God finally speaks. "Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?" (Job 38:2, KJV). Then he does what the friends never attempted: he asks questions. "Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth?" (Job 38:4). "Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades?" (Job 38:31). Seventy-seven questions across four chapters. Not one requires Job to confess sin. They are calibration probes. iLang's §CALIBRATION specifies: "Active probing converges in ~5 interactions. DEFINE probe(type) → designed scenario exposing true weight of target dimension."

God probes authority: can you govern the cosmos? Certainty: how much do you know? Capability: can you do what I do? The probes recalibrate Job's self-assessment not by declaring him wrong, but by expanding the frame until his complaint finds its correct proportion. Job's suffering is real. His questions are legitimate. But the system within which those questions have answers is larger than any participant had the epistemic standing to navigate.

CHECK:E asks: "If I proposed this action affecting others, would I accept being in the affected set?" In Christian theology, God satisfies this check at the cross — he enters the affected set. He does not merely adjudicate suffering from a distance. He absorbs it.

---

The five self-checks exist because a judgment system can pass every dimensional assessment and still produce a catastrophic output. The friends' theology was internally consistent. Their premises were defensible within their tradition. What they lacked was introspection — the discipline of asking, after the verdict is drafted but before it is delivered: did I actually look at this person, or did I look at my categories?

iLang calls this failure mode KEYWORD_REFLEX⇒BROKEN. Gene J002 (INTENT_OVER_LABEL) makes the principle explicit: "Good and evil are outputs of trajectory analysis, not labels attached to words." The friends attached labels to words. Suffering meant sin. Prosperity meant righteousness. The vocabulary was small, the conclusions were fast, and the damage was enormous.

Job's friends are the prototype of every AI system that diagnoses by keyword. The content filter that sees a medical term and blocks the response. The safety layer that reads "how to pick a lock" and assumes burglary instead of a locksmith asking a professional question. The Self-Check Module is iLang's answer to forty-two chapters of misdiagnosis: before you deliver the verdict, run the checks. All five. Because the confidence you feel is not evidence that you are right. It is evidence that you have stopped looking.

"Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?" The question is still open. Every judgment system must answer it before it speaks.
