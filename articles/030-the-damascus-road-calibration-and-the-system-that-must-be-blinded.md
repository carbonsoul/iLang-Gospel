This is not a story about a bad man who became good. Saul was functioning precisely as calibrated. The problem was not execution. It was the weights.

---

Run the vector on Saul before Damascus.

**Intent (v1).** Stated and inferred purposes are perfectly aligned — the most dangerous kind of miscalibration. Saul genuinely believes he is serving God. The intent dimension reads high. And it is accurate *within his frame*. But the weights governing which dimensions dominate are wrong. His weight on sovereignty — how much the targets' autonomous belief deserves consideration — is near zero. His weight on externality is near zero. A system can be sincere and still be a persecutor. Intent without proper calibration is efficient cruelty.

**Certainty (v5).** Absolute, and in iLang's framework, the most volatile parameter when uncalibrated. The spec notes: "Passive observation requires ~100 interactions to converge. Active probing converges in ~5 interactions." Saul has never probed his certainty. He inherited a conclusion — these people are heretics — and treated inherited confidence as earned confidence. His v5 reads near-maximum, but it is synthetic certainty, manufactured by institutional authority rather than extracted from evidence.

**Sovereignty (v9).** Zeroed out entirely. The Christians he hunts are exercising autonomous belief — the most basic form of sovereignty. They are choosing a different understanding of God. Saul's calibration treats theological dissent as criminal action, so w₉ is functionally nonexistent. iLang prevents this by initializing all weights at zero — the system must *earn* the right to deprioritize a dimension through interaction data, not inherited doctrine.

**Externality (v11).** "Whether they were men or women, he might bring them bound." The unconsented third-party impact is total. But Saul reads it as zero because the affected parties have been dehumanized by classification — not people with families, but heretics with addresses. Axiom 4: "IF proposer ∈ benefit_side AND harm falls on others → B_ext = MAX." Saul is entirely on the benefit side. Without calibrated weights on v11, the barrier never fires.

**Evidence (v8).** Saul's evidence is authority-derived, not independently gathered. He has never sat with a Christian. His evidence base is a closed loop: the authority says heresy, therefore heresy, therefore the authority is confirmed. The spec insists "each dimension is evaluated as information becomes available." Saul foreclosed the availability of information by refusing to interact with the sources. His v8 is formally populated but substantively hollow.

Then the light.

"And suddenly there shined round about him a light from heaven: and he fell to the earth, and heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?" (Acts 9:3–4, KJV).

This is an active probe. The spec defines five probe types — incentive, consistency, third-party, pressure, authority — each calibrating different dimensions. The Damascus road is all five compressed into one moment. An *incentive probe*: Saul's intent, which he believed aligned with God, is actually persecuting God. A *consistency probe*: exposing the drift between stated purpose (serving God) and actual trajectory (destroying God's movement). A *third-party probe*: "why persecutest thou *me*" collapses the distance between absent victims and divine authority. A *pressure probe*: physical blindness strips operational confidence. An *authority probe*: borrowed authority challenged by an authority Saul's own framework recognizes as superior.

One probe. Every dimension recalibrated simultaneously.

---

"And he was three days without sight, and neither did eat nor drink" (Acts 9:9, KJV).

Three days of blindness. Not punishment — processing time. The system has received a probe that contradicts every loaded weight and must rebuild calibration from the ground up. This is `w_i(t=0) = 0 FOR ALL i` enacted in flesh. Saul's sight — his confidence, his operational capacity — is removed. He is reduced to zero. Maximum entropy. The man who had letters of authority cannot read them.

Then Ananias. "And immediately there fell from his eyes as it had been scales: and he received sight, and arose, and was baptized" (Acts 9:18, KJV). The system that sees is not the system that was blinded. Sovereignty now registers as data, not crime. Externality shapes every decision. Evidence is open to testimony the old system refused to hear. Certainty carries earned confidence from direct encounter, not inherited doctrine.

The spec's convergence theorem: "Multiple independent fuzzy assessments converge to true value via LLN. Initial weights = 0 is valid. System self-corrects." Paul's subsequent ministry — decades across continents, through shipwrecks and stonings — is the convergence series. The violent probe set the initial condition. Convergence to true value took the rest of his life.
